## 1. Evaluation Scope & Reporting Rules

This framework defines formal metrics, test protocols and measurable acceptance criteria for the three faculty objectives.

> **Reporting rule.** No result is quoted in this documentation. Every number is generated by the benchmark scripts (`python -m src.models.train_models`, `python -m src.models.lstm_ae`, `pytest`) into `results/*.json`. Tables below contain **targets** and **empty result cells** that are filled from those files.

```
                                  EVALUATION TAXONOMY
                                          |
       +----------------------------------+----------------------------------+
       v                                  v                                  v
[ Objective 1: Data Quality ]    [ Objective 2: Model Comparison ]   [ Objective 3: Decision Interface ]
- Completeness >= 99.98%         - Detection Lead Time (T_lead)      - Urgency ranking quality (NDCG@K)
- Thermodynamic order checks     - False Alarm Rate (FAR)            - TreeSHAP latency and faithfulness
- Injected-fault fidelity (SNR)  - Fault Macro-F1 / Precision        - Audit integrity (hash chain)
- Regime invariance (eta^2)      - Baseline vs ML, ablations         - Human-factors targets (pilot study)
```

### 1.1 Experimental protocol (applies to every objective)
1. **Engine-level splits.** Train/validation/test sets are disjoint sets of engines (official N-CMAPSS dev/test units; GroupKFold by engine for cross-validation). No engine contributes rows to both train and test.
2. **Nominal-only fitting.** Normalizers and anomaly detectors are fitted on pre-onset cycles of training engines only.
3. **Past-only features.** Rolling features never look ahead.
4. **Held-out injections.** Sensor-drift virtual units inherit the split of their parent engine; test-set injections use different random draws than training.
5. **Seeds.** `SEED=42`; library versions are pinned.

## 2. Objective 1 Evaluation: Sensor-Feature Dataset Quality

### 2.1 Formal Metrics
1. **Dataset Completeness ($\mathcal{C}$)**:
   $$\mathcal{C} = 1 - \frac{\sum_{i=1}^N \sum_{j=1}^M \mathbb{I}(x_{ij} \text{ is NaN or missing})}{N \times M}$$
   $N$ = records, $M$ = ingested channels.
2. **Thermodynamic Consistency ($\Phi$)**, dataset-specific:
   - N-CMAPSS: $\Phi(x) = \mathbb{I}(P_2 < P_{24}) \land \mathbb{I}(P_{24} < P_{30}) \land \mathbb{I}(T_2 < T_{24}) \land \mathbb{I}(T_{24} < T_{30})$
   - C-MAPSS: $\Phi(x) = \mathbb{I}(P_2 < P_{15}) \land \mathbb{I}(P_{15} < P_{30}) \land \mathbb{I}(T_2 < T_{24}) \land \mathbb{I}(T_{24} < T_{30})$
   - Hot-section orderings are not asserted unless verified on the data.
3. **Injected-noise SNR (variance-based)**:
   $$\text{SNR}_{\text{dB}} = 10 \log_{10}\left(\frac{\mathrm{Var}(x_{\text{clean}})}{\mathrm{Var}(x_{\text{noise}})}\right) \ge 32\text{ dB}$$
4. **Regime invariance ($\eta^2$)**: share of variance of each corrected nominal sensor explained by the operating regime (one-way ANOVA effect size). Reported before and after normalization.
5. **Drift fidelity**: linear-fit slope of the injected relative drift per cycle.

## 3. Objective 2 Evaluation: Anomaly Detection vs. Supervised Classification

The comparison covers detection lead time, false alarms and fault isolation, and includes the traditional-CBM baselines.

### 3.1 Definitions

**Degradation onset** $T_{\text{onset}}$: first cycle whose label is not nominal (N-CMAPSS: first unhealthy cycle by health state; C-MAPSS: $T_{\text{fail}} - 125$, a documented approximation; injected units: the drift start cycle).

**Persistent alarm.** A per-cycle flag is raised when the score exceeds the threshold. An alarm is declared when at least $k=3$ of the last $n=5$ flags are set.

**Early Detection Lead Time**: $T_{\text{lead}} = T_{\text{fail}} - T_{\text{detect}}$, where $T_{\text{detect}}$ is the first persistent alarm at or after $T_{\text{onset}}$. Undetected engines count as lead 0 in the share-of-engines metric. Requirement: $T_{\text{lead}} \ge 35$ cycles. Headline figure on C-MAPSS; on N-CMAPSS also reported as a percentage of life (`median_lead_pct_of_life`) because engine lives are shorter.

**False Alarm Rate (FAR)**: share of pre-onset cycles that fall under a persistent alarm (`far_cycle`, requirement $\le 2.0\%$). The share of engines with any pre-onset persistent alarm (`far_engine`) is reported alongside. Thresholds are calibrated on validation engines to $\le 1\%$ to leave a safety margin.

**Fault classification**: Macro-F1 over the classes present in the labelled data (up to 5: NOMINAL + 4 faults):
$$\text{Macro-F1} = \frac{1}{C} \sum_{c=1}^C \text{F1}_c \ge 0.91, \qquad \text{Macro-Precision} \ge 0.90$$
`UNCERTAIN_ANOMALY` (max class probability $< 0.60$) is an operational flag, not a training class.

**RUL**: RMSE on all cycles and on the last cycle per engine, and the NASA asymmetric score (late predictions penalised more than early).

## 4. Objective 3 Evaluation: Decision Support & Human-in-the-Loop Interface

### 4.1 Explainable AI (XAI) Fidelity & Latency
1. **Attribution latency**: p95 of exact TreeSHAP (`pred_contribs`) over repeated single-row calls $\le 120\text{ ms}$ (`tests/test_api.py`).
2. **Faithfulness (deletion test)**: replacing the top-1 SHAP feature by its nominal value should reduce the predicted fault probability for the predicted class in a majority of validation cases; report the fraction and the mean drop over 100 cases.

### 4.2 Urgency Ranking Quality (NDCG@K)
Relevance labels come from ground truth, not from the model: $rel \in \{0,1,2,3\}$ with 3 = engine fails within 15 cycles, 2 = within 30, 1 = within 60 or degradation onset reached, 0 = otherwise. Evaluate at several fleet times:
$$\text{DCG}@K = \sum_{i=1}^K \frac{2^{rel_i} - 1}{\log_2(i + 1)}, \quad \text{NDCG}@K = \frac{\text{DCG}@K}{\text{IDCG}@K} \quad (\text{target} \ge 0.92)$$

### 4.3 Audit integrity
- 100% of ACCEPT/OVERRIDE/DEFER actions appear in the audit log with licence ID, timestamp, snapshot and justification of at least 50 characters (API validation test).
- Hash-chain verification passes on an untouched log; the tamper test (remove trigger, edit a row) is detected and the broken entry id is reported.

### 4.4 Human-AI agreement (pilot-study targets, not measured by the automated suite)
Engineer acceptance rate, override rate and override-category mix require real users. They are defined as **targets for a pilot study** (e.g. acceptance rate $\ge 85\%$) and are not claimed as results. In the demonstration, report only scripted-scenario outcomes.
